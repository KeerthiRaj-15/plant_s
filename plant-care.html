PLANT CARE GUIDE

Keep your
plants thriving.

Simple routines for light, watering, feeding and seasonal care.

START HERE

Four habits that matter most.

Use these basics before adding complicated routines.

01

Check the light

Match the plant to the amount of natural light your room actually receives. Rotate pots occasionally for even growth.

02

Water with intention

Check the soil before watering. Avoid keeping roots permanently saturated unless the plant specifically prefers wet conditions.

03

Watch the leaves

Leaves can signal stress. Yellowing, crisp edges or drooping are reasons to check light, moisture and drainage.

04

Feed in season

Use suitable fertilizer during active growth and ease back when growth naturally slows.

QUICK ANSWERS

Plant care FAQ

How often should I water?

There is no universal schedule. Check soil moisture and the plant's light and temperature before watering.

Should I repot immediately?

Not always. Give a new plant time to acclimate unless the pot or drainage is clearly unsuitable.

What is the easiest indoor plant?

Start with resilient varieties such as Pothos and Philodendron and choose a location with suitable light.
